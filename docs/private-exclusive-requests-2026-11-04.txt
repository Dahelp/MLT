Four optional selections appear immediately below the travel/date fields.
They have no prices and do not affect the public Base package or Total.
The confirmation dialog lists the requests; the account draft carries the
catalog IDs through sign-in/registration into the authenticated booking.
The existing one-active-journey policy for ordinary travellers is retained.

The selected requests are stored separately in mlt_orders.exclusive_requests_json.
Null price means awaiting a quote; zero is an explicitly quoted free service.
Selections cannot be added or removed by the pricing endpoint. Only the existing
concierge and admin booking workspaces may assign prices, in the application card.
The existing manager chat role and its access boundaries are preserved.

Saving a complete individual quote updates the saved journey total and the
client account, with an audit record and quote revision. Base package, refundable
security and the first two Private payment stages remain unchanged. The quoted
supplement is included in the final payment. Repeated saves do not compound it.
Quote edits are refused during an active checkout and for paid/closed journeys;
the total cannot fall below money already received. Route/note edits preserve
the selection and cannot overwrite its quote.
